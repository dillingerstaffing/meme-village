MODEL

Ten residents. They work, rest, help, feud, and copy each other, live. You direct the work and choose who gets watched. Nobody reads paragraphs here, including you.

📚 Storehouse. Staffed shelves make fairness visible: trust.
🎓 School. Teaching builds cohesion and autonomy.

🌿 Fields. Food work pushes scarcity down fastest.
⚙ Mill. Goods work: steady productivity.
🌴 The Green. Social time: morale, cohesion, and copying.

Bars over heads: green is morale, blue is energy. A dashed gold ring is a model being watched. Your orders override a resident's own ideas, and the village notices how often that happens: that is the Autonomy meter. Everything else, the village will show you.
