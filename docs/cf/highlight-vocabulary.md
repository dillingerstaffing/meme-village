# One Highlight, One Meaning in the Selection Panel

The selection panel's visual treatments are a vocabulary, and each
treatment means exactly one thing:

- Teal (.on): the selected resident's current order. Only order
  buttons (the Send to group, Build included) may carry it, and at
  most one is lit at a time.
- Gold border (.armed): the one armed verb waiting for its target
  tap. Arming any verb disarms every other, the Cover chooser
  included; at most one gold border exists on screen.
- Gold flash (.rdyflash): a cooldown just expired. Spotlight only.
- Dashed border (.cam): the camera follows the selected resident.
  Follow only. Follow's face also names its state: Following while
  the camera follows, Follow when it does not.
- Dimmed: temporarily unavailable. The reason or the countdown
  rides on the button's face, or the tap itself explains why the
  verb cannot act yet (Speak For, Build); a verb never disables
  silently.
- The 💭 badge on an order button: that station matches the
  resident's want. A badge, never a border treatment or a pulse.
- The 📜 badge on an order button: that station is the season's
  accepted, unfinished commission station, the one whose driven work
  counts toward the commission. A badge, never a border treatment
  or a pulse. It reads at the moment of choice, on the button the
  choice is made with, instead of only on the objective card, the
  minimap, and the in-world ring. It clears when the commission
  completes or is set aside, and the tray diff key carries the
  commission state so it moves or clears without a forced refresh.
  The two badges can share one button: the want badge rides the
  top-right corner, the commission badge the top-left, so neither
  covers the other.

- The NEW badge (.nb): a verb the latest promotion just unlocked.
  A small gold NEW tag in the button's top-right corner, in the
  want/commission badge grammar, on the Claim and Road buttons from
  the Town promotion until the first tap on each verb (tray button
  or keyboard twin). A badge, never a border treatment or a pulse.
  The promotion toast names the unlock too, but a toast is transient
  and the promotion lands in the busiest toast queue of the game, so
  the badge is the persistent half of the teaching: the verb itself
  marks its arrival until the player has seen it.

Status is never a lit button: sickness, tier, childhood, and the
watched state render as read-only chips in the info block. Sound's
face also names its state: Muted while muted, Sound while sounding,
beside its icon swap, so the one toggle that silences every other
answer still reads in words. Buttons
are verbs only. Resident verbs group as Send to, Influence, and
Care; village-scope controls (Follow, Proclaim, Found, Story,
Sound) sit in their own separated cluster and never mix into the
resident groups. Cover is picked from priced chips that name the
tier, its per-season premium, and what the tier buys before the
committing tap; it is never cycled blind. Call Out follows the same rule in the same Care group: its button names
what the fee buys (the front of the medic queue) beside the fee before the one-tap commit, never the fee alone.

Why: in the 2026-10-22 playtest the single teal treatment meant six
different things at once (an order, a camera toggle, a mute state,
four armed intents) and gold meant three more, so several elements
stayed lit with unrelated meanings and none of them read. The
player could not tell state from action, or an armed verb from a
standing one.
